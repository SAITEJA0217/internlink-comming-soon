OUR STORY

About InternLink

By students, for the global student community.

We started InternLink because we noticed something simple. Many students struggle to move from college learning to real-world experience. Like many of you, we were students who found it really hard to find our first internships. We felt that many job sites were too corporate and didn't really help beginners who are just starting out.

As a small team of students, we decided to build a solution that we actually wanted to use. We wanted a place that wasn't just about job listings, but also about the tools and guidance we needed to grow. That's why InternLink focuses on providing clear career paths and helpful resources that matter to students today.

Our mission is simple: we want to help students find good internships, no matter which college they go to or where they live. We are still a growing platform, and we are working hard every day to add more value for our early users. Whether you're a coder, a designer, or an AI learner, we're here to help you get started.
